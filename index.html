Infrastructure for computational research.

Tools and systems for running, evaluating and studying computational workloads.

A narrow scope, worked deeply.

Counfield builds infrastructure for data-intensive research and model workloads. The work concentrates on three areas: data systems, model infrastructure and evaluation.

We keep the scope narrow on purpose. A small organization works on a small number of problems, and each one is worked until it is useful.

Public work will appear on this site when it is ready, with its documentation.
