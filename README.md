## What we are

OGMA is a frontier AI lab focused on **multimodal agent systems** — software that sees, reasons, and acts. We build models, runtimes, and developer tools that turn screens, images, and visual documents into structured work.

We believe the most useful AI in the next decade will be the kind that meets a human halfway: it watches what the user sees, understands the task, plans a route through it, and reports back honestly when it cannot. Not autonomous, not flashy, not magic — just genuinely helpful at the boundary between perception and action.

---

## What we are building

A small family of products around a single thesis: **vision is the missing API for software.** Everything a developer, an analyst, or an end-user touches has a visual surface — a screen, a document, a dashboard, a chart. Making that surface programmable, without scraping, without brittle automation, and without sending sensitive data to opaque third parties — that is the problem space we are committing to.

Today we are working on:

### Ogma Optron — *open-source visual task understanding runtime*

A self-hostable runtime that takes a screenshot or an image and produces a structured task plan, then walks it through specialized agents to a grounded markdown report. Includes a safe, read-only code-agent module for repository-aware reasoning.

- Public: **<https://github.com/berkmec/ogma-optron>**
- License: MIT
- Status: MVP shipped; entering an extended dev-tool expansion phase (CLI, semantic repo intelligence, sandboxed browser agent, deepthink reasoning).

### Additional models and runtimes — *in development*

We are working on additional model families and runtimes. Details are intentionally not public yet — we will announce them when they are ready to ship.

## Our vision

We want OGMA to be the home of:

- **Honest AI** — systems that say "I don't know" instead of inventing answers, that quote evidence verbatim instead of paraphrasing it into something convenient.
- **Self-hostable AI** — first-class open-source products developers can run on their own infrastructure with their own credentials, not just SaaS dashboards we control.
- **Composable AI** — model and provider choice as a feature, not as lock-in. We do not believe the future of this industry is one vendor and one endpoint.
- **Practical AI** — products people actually use to finish work, not benchmarks we win on a leaderboard and never ship.

This is a long arc. We are early.
