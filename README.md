## What we do

Counfield builds tools and systems for running, evaluating and studying computational workloads.

The work concentrates on three areas:

- **Data systems**
- **Model infrastructure**
- **Evaluation**

We keep the scope narrow on purpose. A small organization works on a small number of problems, and each one is worked until it is useful.

## Public work
